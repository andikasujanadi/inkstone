# Contributor permission checklist

This is a maintainer workflow, not an executed CLA or a grant from any contributor.

## Existing contribution

PR #1 by andikasujanadi, commit `f19aa0903aed00e6a84bc3e4e86d42cd712f73cc`, is credited in the licensing history. No copyright assignment or separate commercial permission is presumed. This preview continues the existing source-available distribution and retains attribution.

Before a future commercial software release containing that contribution, obtain and retain the applicable written permission or agreement. Record:

- The contributor and the exact pull request/commits covered.
- The permitted uses, including any intended paid app-store distribution, modifications and redistribution.
- Any required sublicensing or distribution under additional terms.
- Attribution requirements and any exclusions or third-party material.
- The contributor's confirmation that they can provide the specified grant.
- The agreed text, acceptance date and a private location for the permission record.

If the scope is uncertain, resolve it before commercial distribution. Do not mark this checklist complete without evidence. No permission request was sent and no new grant was obtained as part of this release.

## Future contributions

Review rights and attribution before merging code. If the project's intended distribution needs an additional agreement, settle that agreement explicitly rather than assuming that the public repository license transfers ownership or grants broader rights. A suitable reviewed CLA or a specific written permission can be recorded here by reference once actually accepted.
